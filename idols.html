Two voices.

Some artists become more than names on a screen. Their voices, movement, presence, and stories become part of the memories people carry with them. This space is dedicated to two icons who continue to inspire Cedric — Whitney Houston and Michael Jackson.

Different stories.

Whitney Houston and Michael Jackson created in different ways, but both left behind something that cannot be measured only by records or performances: a feeling. Their work reminds us that art can become memory, comfort, inspiration, and a language of its own.

Whitney and Michael remind us that an artist's life may have an ending, but the meaning people find in the art can continue. Years can pass, generations can change, and still a familiar voice or a familiar rhythm can bring a memory back to life.

This is not only a collection of pictures. It is a small corner of Cedric's universe reserved for admiration, inspiration, and gratitude for two artists whose work continues to move people.
